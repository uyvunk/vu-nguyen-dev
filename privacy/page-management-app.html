Privacy Policy

Facebook - Page Management App · Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy covers Facebook - Page Management App, a private tool operated by Vu Nguyen (The Site Handyman) to manage the Facebook Pages he runs, such as Bà Tám Kể Chuyện. It is not offered to the public, and it has no Facebook Login for other people.

What the app does

  • Publishes posts and Stories to the Pages it manages.
  • Reads engagement numbers on those Pages' own posts (reactions, comments, shares, followers).
  • Reads comments left on those Pages' own posts so the Page can reply to them, or hide spam.

Information we process

When you comment on a post by a Page this app manages, the app may read the text of your comment, the comment's ID and time, and your name as Facebook provides it. It does not access your profile, friends, messages, or anything outside comments on that Page's posts.

How it is used

  • To draft a reply to your comment, or to hide comments that are spam or abusive.
  • Comment text is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service to draft replies and sort comments. It is not used for advertising.
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone else.

Storage and retention

Comment records are stored on the operator's own computer, only so the same comment isn't handled twice. They are kept for as long as the Page runs, or deleted sooner if you ask.

How to delete your data

Email vunguyenk@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data – Page Management App", and include your Facebook name, the Page, or a link to your comment. We will delete every record about you within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also delete your own comment on Facebook at any time.
